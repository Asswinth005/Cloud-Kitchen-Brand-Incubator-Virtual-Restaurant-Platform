Core Purpose

Our Mission

To democratize food entrepreneurship by providing world-class kitchen infrastructure, brand incubation, and delivery technology — so anyone with a great recipe can build a thriving food business.

We believe the future of dining is decentralized, delivery-first, and driven by culinary creativity — not by who can afford a prime corner location.

Future Outlook

Our Vision

A world where every neighborhood has access to diverse, high-quality food brands — and where talented chefs can launch and scale without barriers.

By 2030, we aim to operate 100 kitchen hubs across North America, incubating 500+ virtual brands and creating thousands of culinary jobs.

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

From a single kitchen concept in Chicago to powering dozens of brands nationwide.

2021 Chicago, IL

Founded in Chicago

Two chefs and one small commercial kitchen. Our first virtual brand, Tandoor Trail, launched with 12 orders on day one.

2022 3 Hubs

First 10 Brands

Expanded to 3 kitchen hubs and welcomed our first 10 partner brands. Launched our proprietary kitchen management platform.

2024 Series A

Series A Funding

Raised $12M in Series A funding to accelerate expansion. Grew to 12 kitchen hubs across 6 states and 30+ active virtual brands.

2026 1M+ Meals

1 Million Meals Delivered

Celebrated 1M+ meals delivered. Now operating 18 kitchen hubs with 42 virtual brands — and building the future of dining.
